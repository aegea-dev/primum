---
title: primum show
description: One item with its scores, needs, text and documents.
---

```bash
primum show 87
primum show 87 --json
```

Prints the item's id and title, its scores, priority and status, its file,
then its text and the documents beside it.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| `--json` | The item as JSON, the same shape as one element of `list --json` |
